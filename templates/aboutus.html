Our Purpose & Methodology

Bridging Clinical Knowledge & Modern Artificial Intelligence

MEDiWiSE was designed to empower individuals with early diagnostic screening, accessible triage tools, and safe wellness guidance during moments of uncertainty.

Multilabel Machine Learning

Trained on over 4,900 verified clinical cases, our Random Forest classifier maps overlapping symptoms to specific disease likelihoods without relying on simple keyword matching.

Model: RF Ensemble (131 features)

PyTorch Computer Vision

EfficientNet-B0 architecture balances speed and classification accuracy, processing 224x224 RGB image tensors to detect skin conditions across 7 distinct classes.

Architecture: EfficientNet-B0

Generative Intelligence

Powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, the conversational layer delivers empathetic, clear, non-clinical explanations and natural self-care remedies.

LLM: gemini-2.5-flash
Clinical Transparency

Our Responsible AI Philosophy

MEDiWiSE was designed from the ground up under Dev Bhoomi Uttarakhand University with a strict ethical commitment: artificial intelligence should clarify, triage, and support, but must never masquerade as a licensed medical practitioner.

Every diagnostic screen provides confidence scores and prominently directs patients toward physical medical consultation and 108 emergency services when severe symptoms present themselves.
